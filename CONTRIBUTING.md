## Contribution expectations

- Keep changes focused and maintainable.
- Do not submit code or assets copied from projects whose licenses are
  incompatible with oneView's licensing model.
- Clearly identify third-party code or assets and their license when inclusion
  is necessary.
- Do not submit confidential material or material you are not authorized to
  publish.
